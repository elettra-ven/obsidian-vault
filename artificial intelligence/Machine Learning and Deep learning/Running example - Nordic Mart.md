## Business goals in play today
* reduce churn
* personalize offers
* forecast demand
* catch fraud

## 1. Analytics

![[Pasted image 20260917153709.png|637]]

The process must begin from a business question, like:
- *Which customers are about to stop buying from us?*
- *How much will this customer spend next quarter?*
- *What are our natural customer segments?* 
- *What products get bought together?*   
- Which transactions look fraudulent?
These tasks can be traced back to [[Classification]], [[Regression]], [[Clustering]], Association rules, etc ...
