>The word metabolism comes from the Greek ***metabolé*** and means change or transformation.

Living organisms can either be classified as **autotrophs** or **heterotrophs**. 

>[!info] Autotrophs
>These organims, such as plants, operate their metabolism through **photosynthesis**:
>$$CO_2 + H_2O \rightarrow O_2 + \text{sugars}$$
>This process needs the addition of **energy**, which is extracted from the sunlight.

>[!info] Heterotrophs
>Their metabolic function follows the **respiration**:
>$$O_2 + \text{sugars} \rightarrow CO_2 + H_2O$$
>This cycle produces energy for the organism's body.

## Cycling of carbon 
Sugars/organic products and $CO_2$ are borrowed by both cycles and cycle constantly through the biosphere. 
This flow of mass is enormous ($4*10^{11}$ **metric tons** of carbon turned annually).

## Catabolism and Anabolism

Cellular metabolism...
1. produces **energy** necessary for vital functions of the cell. 
	These pathways are called ==**catabolic**==.
	They degrade nutrients into simpler products to extract chemical energy.
2. **synthesizes** the building blocks required for **growth** and **maintenance**.
	These pathways are called ==**anabolic**==.
	The convert small molecules into larger and more complex structures.
	They require the input of energy.

>[!warning] The activation of one mode (**C/A**) is accompanied by reciprocal inhibition of the other mode.

The presence of several steps within the pathways provides high flexibility and sophisticated regulation of the overall metabolism.

>[!info] Catabolic pathways
>These pathways **converge** in order to produce few molecules.
>![[img/Pasted image 20260930111135.png]]

>[!info] Anabolic pathways
>These pathways **diverge** in order to produce many and diverse molecules.

Metabolic pathways are **regulated** at several levels:
- **Regulation by availability of substrate**
  It regulates the metabolite concentration during production, uptake, etc ...
- **Regulation by activation/inhibition of the enzymatic activity**
  For example, phosphorylation
- **Regulation by manipulation of the amount of enzymes**
  Operates directly on gene expression and translation
